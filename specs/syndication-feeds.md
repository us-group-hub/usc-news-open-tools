# USC.NEWS Public Syndication & Indexing Endpoints

| Endpoint | Standard | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `https://usc.news/feed/` | RSS 2.0 + Media RSS + WebSub (`pubsubhubbub`) | Real-time feed readers, aggregators, and news apps |
| `https://usc.news/substack-feed.xml` | Full-HTML RSS 2.0 (`content:encoded`) | Newsletter & canonical syndication importers |
| `https://usc.news/news-sitemap.xml` | Google News Sitemap 0.9 + Image Sitemap 1.1 | Fresh news discovery with `>=1200px` 16:9 hero imagery |
| `https://usc.news/sitemap_index.xml` | XML Sitemap Index | Full site discovery |
