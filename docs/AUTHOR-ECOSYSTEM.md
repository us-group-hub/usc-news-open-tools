# USC.NEWS Author Profiles, Subscriptions & Contributor Monetization (`/u/`)

USC.NEWS combines independent editorial coverage with an open contributor publishing platform:

1. **E-E-A-T Author Profile Pages:** Every writer receives a structured profile showcasing verified bio, publication archive, subscriber count, and dedicated author RSS feed.
2. **One-Click Author Subscriptions & Notification Bell:** Readers can subscribe to specific analysts and receive instant on-site bell alerts plus scheduled email digests whenever a followed author publishes new research.
3. **Visual Frontend Publishing (`/my-posts/` & `/dashboard/`):** Contributors compose rich articles directly on the frontend with a floating `+` block menu, inline image uploads, YouTube/Vimeo embeds, and automatic hero cover extraction.
4. **Transparent Contributor Monetization (`/u/`):**
   - Verified contributors earn reward points directly into their `/u/` balance for publishing original quality articles (`+10` per published article) and inviting new writers via their personal referral link (`+10` per referred contributor).
   - Strict anti-spam penalties (`-100` for spam violations) protect publication quality.
   - Full public reward ledger history and direct non-custodial withdrawal support are visible on [`https://usc.news/u/`](https://usc.news/u/).
